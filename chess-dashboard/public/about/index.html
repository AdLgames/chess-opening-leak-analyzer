OPENING LEAK LAB

About & sources

A chess repertoire should be something you understand, remember and use.

An independent learning project

Opening Leak Lab is developed by AdLgames. It combines guided opening lessons, a move explorer, saved repertoires, scheduled recall and optional analysis of your own games. You can start with a board lesson before creating an account.

We focus on players who know the rules and want a clearer opening plan. Three authored courses cover the Italian Game for White, the Caro–Kann and the Queen’s Gambit Declined for Black. Reference opening guides and database statistics cover a broader set of positions; they are not all full training courses.

Where the chess content comes from

Course explanations: fixed, authored learning notes attached to specific positions. Legal moves, course lines and teaching-diagram attacks are checked with python-chess. Automated legality checks do not establish that an explanation is the only good plan.

Static teaching boards are rendered with python-chess and use piece artwork by Colin M. L. Burnett, as distributed through python-chess under GPL-3.0-or-later. Attribution and source · Licence. The live interactive board uses this project’s original piece art.

Opening statistics: a book derived from public Lichess game data (CC0). Database pages show sample counts, the side whose score is measured, and selection thresholds. A score includes half a point for a draw. Observed results are not engine evaluations or proof of a move’s quality.

Computer analysis: Stockfish, distributed under GPLv3. Concrete engine output depends on search limits and is labelled separately from authored lessons. The repository includes the installer and licence information.

Illustrated guides: Maya, Theo and Inez are fictional characters with fixed, authored reactions. They are not human coaches, titled players or a live advice service. Their artwork was generated for this project; asset provenance is recorded in the repository.

What progress means here

Introducing a position, recalling a move without help and playing a practical continuation are separate records. Hints and retries are useful practice, but do not count as first-attempt independent recall. We do not translate these records into an invented rating gain or an all-purpose mastery percentage.

Corrections and source code

Found a confusing explanation or a wrong position? Report the exact page and move. The project repository contains the source, tests, dataset notes and licence details. Opening Leak Lab is independent of Chess.com, Lichess and the Stockfish project.
