Something unclear? A move that needs another look? Tell us where you found it.

Report a problem or suggest an improvement

Use the project’s GitHub issues for website bugs, feature suggestions and chess-content corrections. A GitHub account is needed to post. Include the page URL, the move or position, what happened, and what you expected.

Open a project issue →

Issues are public. Do not include private PGNs, email sign-in links, access tokens, account exports or other personal data. For an account-access problem, describe the general issue and request a private follow-up channel before sharing identifying details.

Account data

You can download your saved account data or delete your account from the account menu in your workspace. Signing out and deleting an account are different actions. Read Privacy & your data for what is stored locally and on the hosted service.

Chess feedback

For a content correction, include the guide or course name, move number and the position if possible. Explain whether the problem concerns legality, a missing alternative, an engine continuation or an unclear plan. This helps us reproduce the decision instead of guessing from a game result.
