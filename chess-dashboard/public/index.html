        8 · Engine & data
        
            Everything bundled, nothing phoning home
          
        
          
        
            
        
              
        Bundled engine
        
              
        
              
        
                Stockfish is GPLv3 and ships as a binary in chessopening/bin/; source at
                official-stockfish/Stockfish.
              
        
            
        
            
        
              
        Local opening book
        
              
        
              
        
                Built from monthly dumps at
                database.lichess.org
                (CC0), with ECO names from
                lichess-org/chess-openings
                (CC0). Rebuild any month with tools/build_local_db.py.
              
        
            
        
            
        
              
        How a leak is flagged
        
              
          
                
        • EVAL_DROP Stockfish eval falls by more than your threshold after your move.
          • 
                
        • WINRATE_DECLINE your score in that exact position trails the book by more than the score gap.
          • 
                
        • OFFBEAT_MOVE a move almost nobody plays, and it is not working for you.
          • 
              
        
              
        Priority = points lost + eval drop × games × 0.25.
