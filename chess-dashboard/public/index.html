Book + engine

An engine alone would just tell you everything is fine

Stockfish will happily call your move playable. The book knows that at your level, nobody scores with it. Leak Lab only flags a move when both agree something is wrong — the evaluation drifts and the practical results follow it down.

That is why the list is five lines long instead of fifty. Fixing a repeated opening move is worth more than memorising a new one.
