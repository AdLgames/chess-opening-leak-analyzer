BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Chess, with less friction.

Can I learn chess openings without importing games?

Yes. Play the public board lesson or start a guided course straight away. Importing games is an optional way to find more personal practice positions.

Do I need an account?

No account is needed to try lessons. Guest progress stays on this device when storage is available. An account adds saved account progress and cross-device access.

How many guided courses are there?

Three: the Italian Game for White, and the Caro–Kann and Queen’s Gambit Declined for Black. Together they contain 17 chapters and 59 training positions. The opening explorer covers a broader reference library.
