BEFORE YOUR FIRST MOVE

A few good questions.

Is the Caro–Kann just a passive defence?

No. Its opening moves support a central challenge with …d5. Some lines begin quietly, but Black still needs active development and well-timed breaks; a solid pawn structure does not defend itself.

What should I learn after 1…c6?

Start with 2.d4 d5, then separate White’s main choices: advancing with e5, exchanging on d5, or defending e4 with a knight. Those choices create different plans.

Do I play as Black in the lessons?

Yes. The course board faces Black and the prompts identify the side to move. You practise Black’s responses and can later play a short continuation against Stockfish.
