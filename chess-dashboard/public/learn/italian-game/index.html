BEFORE YOUR FIRST MOVE

A few good questions.

Is the Italian Game suitable for beginners?

Yes, if you already know how the pieces move. Its first moves develop pieces and contest the centre. You still need to notice Black’s replies; it is not a system you can play blindly.

Does this course cover every reply to 1.e4?

No. It focuses on the Italian after 1.e4 e5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.Bc4, plus selected deviations. The Sicilian, French and Caro–Kann lead to different openings.

Are the lessons free to try?

You can play the public example and begin the guided course without creating an account or importing games. Guest learning progress is stored on this device when browser storage is available.
