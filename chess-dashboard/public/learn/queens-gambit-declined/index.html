BEFORE YOUR FIRST MOVE

A few good questions.

Why decline the Queen’s Gambit?

With 2…e6, Black supports the pawn on d5 rather than immediately capturing on c4. This keeps central tension and leads to classical development plans. Accepting the gambit is a different, playable opening.

Can I use this against every 1.d4 opening?

The course begins with 1.d4 d5 2.c4 e6. White can choose other second moves, so the repertoire is not a universal answer to every queen-pawn setup.

What happens to the light-squared bishop?

The pawn on e6 initially restricts the c8 bishop. Black needs a development plan for it; the appropriate route depends on White’s setup and the central pawn exchanges.
